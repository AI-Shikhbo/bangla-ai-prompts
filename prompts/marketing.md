# AI Prompts for Marketing

## Create a campaign brief

```text
Act as a digital marketing strategist. Create a campaign brief for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Include the customer problem, value proposition, key message, content angles, channels, campaign stages, and measurable goals. Keep assumptions clearly labeled.
```

## Create customer personas

```text
Create 3 practical customer personas for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. For each persona include their goals, frustrations, objections, buying triggers, preferred content, and the message most likely to resonate with them. Avoid stereotypes and unsupported demographic assumptions.
```

## Improve an ad concept

```text
Review this advertising concept and improve it for clarity, relevance, and conversion potential. Provide 5 alternative hooks and explain which audience pain point each one addresses. Do not use false urgency or unsupported claims.

Concept:
[PASTE CONCEPT]
```

## SEO content brief

```text
Create an SEO content brief for the topic [TOPIC]. Include search intent, primary topic, related subtopics, questions readers may have, suggested structure, useful examples, and opportunities for internal links. Do not recommend keyword stuffing.
```

## Analyze a competitor page

```text
Analyze the following competitor content from a marketing perspective. Identify its target audience, core promise, strengths, weaknesses, missing information, and opportunities to create a more useful resource. Do not copy its wording.

Content:
[PASTE CONTENT]
```
