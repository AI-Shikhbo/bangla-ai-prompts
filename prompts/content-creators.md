# AI Prompts for Content Creators

## Generate content ideas

```text
Act as a content strategist for [AUDIENCE]. Generate 20 useful content ideas about [TOPIC]. For each idea include a strong hook, suggested format, the problem it solves, and one practical takeaway. Avoid generic ideas and repeated angles.
```

## Write a social post

```text
Write a natural Facebook post about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Start with a strong hook, provide genuinely useful information, keep the language conversational, and finish with one simple call to action. Avoid clickbait and exaggerated claims.
```

## Create a YouTube outline

```text
Create a YouTube video outline about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Include the hook, introduction, main sections, examples, transitions, and conclusion. The viewer should learn something useful in every section.
```

## Repurpose an article

```text
Turn the following article into: 1 LinkedIn post, 1 Facebook post, 5 short social posts, 5 video hooks, and 5 Pinterest title ideas. Keep each format native to its platform and do not simply copy the article.

Article:
[PASTE ARTICLE]
```

## Improve a headline

```text
Give me 15 headline options for the following content. Make them clear, specific, useful, and curiosity-driven without misleading the reader. Include a mix of search-friendly, benefit-driven, and question-based headlines.

Content:
[PASTE CONTENT]
```
