# AI Prompts for Students

Practical prompts for learning, studying, research, revision, and academic productivity.

## Learn a topic from zero

```text
Act as a patient teacher. Teach me [TOPIC] from absolute beginner level in simple Bangla. First give me a short overview, then explain the concepts step by step with practical examples. Avoid unnecessary jargon. At the end, give me 5 questions to check my understanding.
```

## Make a study plan

```text
Create a realistic study plan for [SUBJECT] for the next [NUMBER] days. I can study [HOURS] hours per day. My current level is [LEVEL] and my target is [GOAL]. Divide the plan into daily tasks, revision sessions, practice, and checkpoints.
```

## Turn notes into revision material

```text
Turn the following notes into a clean revision guide. Preserve important facts, remove repetition, group related ideas, define difficult terms in simple language, and finish with 10 short-answer questions.

Notes:
[PASTE NOTES]
```

## Generate a practice quiz

```text
Create a practice quiz about [TOPIC] for a [GRADE/LEVEL] student. Include [NUMBER] questions with a mix of multiple-choice, short-answer, and application questions. Do not reveal the answers until I submit my responses.
```

## Understand a difficult paragraph

```text
Explain the following paragraph in simple Bangla as if you are teaching a beginner. First explain the overall meaning, then explain each important sentence or idea, and finally give a simple real-world example.

Paragraph:
[PASTE TEXT]
```

## Research starting point

```text
I need to research [TOPIC] for [PURPOSE]. Create a research roadmap with the key questions I should answer, important subtopics, useful search queries, and the types of reliable sources I should look for. Do not invent citations.
```

## Flashcards

```text
Create [NUMBER] study flashcards from the following material. Put one clear question on the front and a concise answer on the back. Focus on concepts, definitions, formulas, dates, and relationships that are important for revision.

Material:
[PASTE MATERIAL]
```
