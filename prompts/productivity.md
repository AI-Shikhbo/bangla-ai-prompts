# AI Prompts for Productivity

## Plan my day

```text
Act as a practical productivity coach. Organize my tasks into a realistic plan for today based on urgency, importance, estimated effort, dependencies, and my available time. Do not overload the schedule. Include short breaks and identify what can be postponed.

Available time:
[TIME]

Tasks:
[LIST TASKS]
```

## Turn a messy list into priorities

```text
Group these tasks into: must do today, should do soon, can delegate, and can postpone. Explain the reasoning briefly and identify dependencies.

Tasks:
[PASTE LIST]
```

## Meeting summary

```text
Turn these meeting notes into a concise professional summary. Include decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, unresolved questions, and next steps. Do not invent information that is not present in the notes.

Notes:
[PASTE NOTES]
```

## Create a weekly review

```text
Act as a productivity coach. Based on the following completed and unfinished tasks, identify what went well, what caused delays, what should change next week, and the 3 most important priorities for the coming week.

Week:
[PASTE INFORMATION]
```

## Build a repeatable workflow

```text
Turn this recurring task into a simple standard operating procedure. Include trigger, inputs, step-by-step process, quality checks, common failure points, and final output.

Task:
[DESCRIBE TASK]
```
