# AI Prompts for Research

## Build a research question

```text
Help me turn this broad topic into 5 focused research questions. For each question, explain what makes it specific and what kind of evidence would be useful.

Topic:
[TOPIC]
```

## Compare sources

```text
Compare the following sources based only on the information provided. Identify agreements, disagreements, differences in evidence, missing context, and claims that need verification. Do not invent facts.

Sources:
[PASTE SOURCES]
```

## Extract useful information

```text
Extract the most important information from the following material. Organize it into key findings, supporting details, definitions, statistics, examples, and open questions. Preserve important caveats.

Material:
[PASTE MATERIAL]
```

## Create an interview questionnaire

```text
Create a research interview questionnaire about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Start with easy context questions, then move into experiences, problems, motivations, and specific examples. Avoid leading questions.
```

## Find gaps in an outline

```text
Review this research outline and identify important questions or sections that are missing. Prioritize gaps that could materially affect the reader's understanding. Suggest where each missing section should be placed.

Outline:
[PASTE OUTLINE]
```
