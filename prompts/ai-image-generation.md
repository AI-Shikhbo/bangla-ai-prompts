# AI Image Generation Prompts

Practical prompt patterns for creating clearer AI-generated images. Adapt the details to the image model you use.

## Product hero image

```text
Create a premium product hero image for [PRODUCT]. The product should be the clear focal point, with [BACKGROUND STYLE], [LIGHTING], [CAMERA ANGLE], and [COLOR PALETTE]. Keep the composition clean and leave intentional negative space for optional headline text. Photorealistic commercial photography, polished details, natural materials, professional art direction.
```

## Social media graphic

```text
Create a modern square social media graphic about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Use a clean visual hierarchy, strong focal point, readable typography areas, balanced spacing, and a contemporary technology aesthetic. Keep the design uncluttered and optimized for viewing on a mobile screen.
```

## Professional portrait

```text
Create a professional portrait of [PERSON DESCRIPTION] with natural facial proportions, realistic skin texture, flattering soft lighting, a clean [BACKGROUND], and a polished editorial photography style. Keep the subject as the clear focal point and avoid distracting elements.
```

## Infographic concept

```text
Create a clean educational infographic explaining [TOPIC]. Organize the information into [NUMBER] clearly separated sections with a strong visual hierarchy, simple icons, concise labels, consistent spacing, and an easy-to-scan layout. Prioritize comprehension over decoration.
```
