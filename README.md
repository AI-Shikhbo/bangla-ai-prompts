# Bangla AI Prompts

A practical collection of reusable AI prompts in Bangla and English for students, freelancers, developers, content creators, marketers, and professionals.

This repository is maintained by **AI Shikhbo** as an open resource for people learning and applying AI in practical work.

## Why this repository?

AI tools are powerful, but good results often depend on giving them clear instructions. This repository collects practical prompts that can be adapted for learning, research, writing, coding, design, freelancing, marketing, and productivity.

**Learn AI in Bangla:** https://aishikhbo.com/

AI Shikhbo publishes Bangla AI tutorials, prompt resources, AI tool guides, freelancing resources, study resources, and practical articles.

## Using the prompts effectively

Replace text inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own information. Better results usually come from providing context, constraints, examples, audience, and the exact output format you need.

A useful prompt usually answers:

1. Who should the AI act as?
2. What exactly should it do?
3. What context does it need?
4. What constraints should it follow?
5. What should the final output look like?
