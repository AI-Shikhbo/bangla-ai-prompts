# Bangla AI Prompts

A practical, open collection of reusable AI prompts in **Bangla and English** for students, freelancers, developers, content creators, marketers, and professionals.

Maintained by **AI Shikhbo** as a free resource for learning and applying AI in practical work.

## What you will find here

- Ready-to-adapt prompts for common real-world tasks
- Bangla and English prompt resources
- Student and learning workflows
- Freelancing and client-work prompts
- Coding and debugging prompts
- Writing, content, and social-media prompts
- Marketing and research prompts
- Productivity workflows
- Links to deeper Bangla AI guides, tools, and open-source resources

## Quick start

1. Choose a category below.
2. Open a prompt file.
3. Replace `[BRACKETED]` placeholders with your information.
4. Add useful context, constraints, examples, and your desired output format.
5. Test the result and refine the prompt for your specific task.

## Prompt-writing checklist

A useful prompt normally specifies:

1. **Role** — who the AI should act as.
2. **Task** — exactly what it should do.
3. **Context** — information it needs to understand the situation.
4. **Constraints** — what it must or must not do.
5. **Output** — the exact format you want back.

Don't treat a prompt as magic wording. The best results usually come from providing better context and iterating on the output.
