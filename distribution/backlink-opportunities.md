# AI Shikhbo Distribution & Backlink Plan

This document turns generic backlink-site lists into a practical distribution strategy for AI Shikhbo.

## Principle

Do not mass-submit the same URL, use automated spam comments, or create thin pages only to obtain links. Publish something useful on each platform and link only when the destination genuinely adds context.

## Priority 1 — strongest fit

### GitHub
**Asset:** this repository

Use the repository itself as the main technical/resource asset. Keep links to AI Shikhbo contextual: prompt library, AI tools directory, OSS directory, and specific guides.

### Medium
**Best assets:** original English/Bangla-adapted educational articles and prompt tutorials.

Good topics:
- How to write better AI prompts in 2026
- AI study workflows for students
- Practical ChatGPT prompts for freelancers
- How to use NotebookLM for study
- A practical AI tools stack for creators

Use one or two highly relevant AI Shikhbo links, not a link dump. The Medium article should stand on its own.

### WordPress.com / Blogger
**Best assets:** original tutorials or a focused companion article.

Do not copy the AI Shikhbo article word-for-word. Create a substantially different article and link to the original/deeper guide where useful.

### Instructables
**Best assets:** actual step-by-step AI projects or workflows with screenshots, prompts, inputs, outputs, and reproducible steps.

A good fit is a practical AI workflow for students or creators rather than a generic promotional article.

### SlideShare
**Best assets:** useful presentations such as “AI Prompting for Students” or “AI Tools for Freelancers”. Include source/reading links on the final slide.

## Priority 2 — useful but secondary

### Quora
Answer questions only when you can add substantial information. A link should be a supporting resource, not the answer itself.

### About.me
Use as a legitimate professional profile for the person/brand behind AI Shikhbo. Keep the profile factual and concise.

### Flipboard / Scoop.it
Use for genuine content curation. Save a small number of particularly useful AI Shikhbo articles rather than every page.

### Tumblr
Use only if you are going to maintain an actual visual/resource feed. Avoid a stream of link-only posts.

## Priority 3 — skip unless there is a real audience benefit

Generic social-bookmark sites, automated directory networks, sites advertising “100% dofollow backlinks”, and platforms whose pages are dominated by unrelated promotional submissions should not be a priority.

## Anchor-text policy

Prefer natural anchors such as:

- AI Shikhbo
- this Bangla AI guide
- the AI prompt library
- the AI tools directory
- this NotebookLM guide
- the full guide for students

Avoid repeating exact-match commercial keywords across platforms.

## 30-day publishing cadence

**Week 1:** GitHub improvements + one original Medium article + one useful presentation.

**Week 2:** One original WordPress.com/Blogger companion article + one genuine Quora answer where relevant.

**Week 3:** One practical Instructables workflow + one curated Flipboard/Scoop.it entry.

**Week 4:** Improve the best-performing resource, add examples, and look for relevant communities or publications that might naturally reference it.

Quality and relevance matter more than the raw number of links.
